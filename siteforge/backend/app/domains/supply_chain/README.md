# supply_chain

Procurement for one project: item, vendor, warehouse, and unit-conversion master data; material requirements; purchase requisitions; and the shared approval route.

RFQ, quotations, purchase orders, expediting, goods receipt, inspection, stock, site issue, and invoices are not open. The screen says so. It does not invent those records.

Accepted stock cannot fulfill a requirement until goods receipt and inspection exist. A linked drawing revision that is superseded or withdrawn flags the requirement for review and does not change quantities or cancel a requisition.
