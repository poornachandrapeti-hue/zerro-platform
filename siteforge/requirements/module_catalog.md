# Agreed module catalogue

| Number | Module | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Project Details | Project identity, location, dates, companies, team, disciplines, locations and attachments. |
| 02 | Engineering | Drawing register, planned deliverables, revisions, reviews, comments, construction releases and verified Wrench integration. |
| 03 | Procurement | Requirements, PRs, RFQs, quotations, orders, expediting and supplier invoices; reference Stores receipts. |
| 04 | Construction | Work packages, readiness, releases, daily execution, verified quantities and handover links. |
| 05 | Financials | Budgets, commitments, costs, certification, invoices, advances, retention and settlements; reference contract terms. |
| 06 | Quality | ITPs, inspections, tests, accepted quantities, NCRs, concessions, holds and dossiers. |
| 07 | Safety | Risk assessments, permits, eligibility, incidents, holds, restart and emergency information. |
| 08 | HR | Personnel, employment, deployment, attendance, leave and qualification evidence. |
| 09 | Contracts | Scope, BOQ/rates, obligations, amendments, claims, notices, guarantees and closeout. |
| 10 | Planning | WBS, calendars, dependencies, baselines, targets, resource forecasts and recovery. |
| 11 | Progress | Source-based measures, approved weighting, variance, reports and immutable snapshots. |
| 12 | Stores | Warehouses, receipt, inventory ledger, reservation, issue, return, transfer and reconciliation. |
| 13 | Requirements and Requests | Common intake, routing, line-level approval, fulfillment evidence and confirmation. |
| 14 | Manpower | Demand, crew allocation, availability, operational hours and utilization. |
| 15 | Equipment and Tools | Assets, custody, demand, allocation, usage, maintenance, inspections and hire. |
| 16 | Commissioning and Handover | Systems, prerequisites, tests, punch clearance, certificates and receiving-party acceptance. |
| 17 | Document Control | Controlled revisions, distribution, acknowledgments, transmittals and dispatch evidence. |
| 18 | Project Risks and Issues | Risk assessment, mitigation, realized issues and management escalation. |
| 19 | Change Management | Cross-module impacts and coordinated implementation; owners approve their changes. |
| 20 | Meetings and Actions | Minutes, decisions and shared actions; no implicit external sending. |
| 21 | Site Logistics | Delivery slots, gates, movement passes, unloading and laydown coordination. |

All modules must use project/company/discipline permissions, responsible owners, appropriate review authority, escalation, audit and source-linked integration. Approval of a request or report does not replace domain technical/safety/financial decisions.
