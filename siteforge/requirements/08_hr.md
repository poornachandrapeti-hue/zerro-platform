# 08. HR

Version: 1.0 | Status: Target requirements, not implementation completion

## Purpose and boundary

Maintain personnel identity, employment/project deployment, attendance and eligibility while protecting personal data.

Backend package: `resources`. Read [Shared foundation](00_shared_foundation.md), [Roles](00_roles_and_access.md), [Workflows and alerts](00_workflows_tasks_alerts.md) and [Integration ownership](00_integration_ownership.md) before implementation.

## Responsible roles

HR Manager/Officer owns personnel and attendance corrections. Supervisors submit timesheets within assigned company. PM sees aggregate readiness; Manpower consumes eligibility and availability rather than confidential HR records.

Apply backend project/company/discipline scope. Managerial escalation does not grant domain approval authority.

## Core records and fields

- Personnel: personnel code, name, employer/company, category, trade, employment dates, contact and optional login link.
- Restricted profiles: identity documents, medical/fitness evidence, bank/payroll references and access policies.
- Deployment: project/company/trade, dates, shift/calendar, supervisor and status.
- Attendance/timesheet: personnel/date/shift, hours, overtime, leave/absence, source, approval and corrections; qualifications/training expiry.

All records also use shared IDs, organization/project scope, owner, version, timestamps and audit links. Attachments reference controlled files; no duplicate file storage.

## Functional requirements

- M08-001: Create employees and contractor workers independently from login users.
- M08-002: Maintain employment/deployment history; prevent conflicting active assignments exceeding availability.
- M08-003: Register trade skills, certificates, induction and eligibility with expiry and verified evidence.
- M08-004: Capture attendance manually or through a configured verified device/file connector; deduplicate imported events.
- M08-005: Submit/review timesheets and overtime under project/company supervisor scope.
- M08-006: Correct approved attendance through authorized revisions with reason and audit.
- M08-007: Manage leave/absence and availability for Manpower planning.
- M08-008: Track onboarding/offboarding, access revocation tasks and return of issued assets.
- M08-009: Provide only eligibility flags to operational modules; mask medical/identity evidence.
- M08-010: Separate personnel access, HR private data, salary/payroll and bank permissions.
- M08-011: Generate authorized payroll input/export where configured; Financials owns payment and payroll accounting integration.
- M08-012: Maintain document expiry reminders and contractor workforce compliance checks.
- M08-013: Respect retention and individual data-access policies defined for deployment.
- M08-014: Produce aggregate headcount/hours by project/company/trade without exposing unnecessary PII.

## Workflow and controls

Personnel: Pre-onboarding → Active → Inactive / Offboarded. Deployment: Proposed → Approved → Active → Ended. Timesheet: Draft → Submitted → Approved / Returned → Corrected Revision.

Validate every transition server-side against current scope, previous state, approval evidence and version. Preserve submitted revisions and decisions. Never allow an unrestricted status dropdown to bypass gates.

## Internal and optional external integrations

- Manpower consumes available/eligible workers and approved hours.
- HSE consumes induction/training/fitness flags; QA consumes qualification flags.
- Equipment/Stores consume asset return tasks; Administration controls login deactivation.
- Financials consumes approved payroll/timesheet export references; no duplicate attendance ledger.

Cross-module writes use owning-domain application interfaces. Use durable events for reporting/alerts, idempotent consumption and reconciliation. External connectors require verified specifications and remain visibly disabled until configured.

## Alerts, escalation and responsibility

- Document/qualification/induction expiry → HR and assigned supervisor; HSE/QA where relevant.
- Missing timesheet/attendance conflict → supervisor then HR Manager.
- Offboarding overdue assets/access → responsible Stores/Equipment/Admin owners.

Each alert rule defines trigger, priority, authorized recipients, SLA/calendar, deduplication and resolution. Apply shared escalation policy; overdue review never auto-approves a record.

## Screens and monitoring

- Personnel/deployment register, attendance review and expiry calendar.
- Authorized workforce summaries and payroll export queue.
- Restricted personnel detail and audit tabs.

Registers support scoped search/filter/sort/pagination and authorized exports. Details show linked source records, evidence, review history, available actions and audit history. Metrics declare source, denominator, unit/currency and refresh time.

## API and implementation contract

Implement scoped list/detail/create/edit endpoints for the records above plus explicit commands for the defined transitions. Define typed input/output schemas, writable-field allowlists, version conflicts and idempotency for postings/handoffs. Add PostgreSQL migrations and permission seeds without granting future capabilities automatically to existing users.

## Acceptance criteria

- AC-08-01: Worker without login can be deployed and counted.
- AC-08-02: Site engineer cannot retrieve private medical/payroll fields.
- AC-08-03: Imported attendance replay does not duplicate hours.
- AC-08-04: Offboarding produces asset/access tasks without deleting history.

- Cross-project, wrong-company, wrong-discipline, revoked-user and unauthorized-file/export tests pass.
- Creation → submission → review → decision → domain application works through real frontend/API flows.
- Duplicate retries and concurrent state transitions preserve quantities, ledgers and audit consistency where applicable.
- Required notification/overdue cases work with a controllable clock and authorized recipients.

## Suggested delivery slices

1. Masters, scoped registers, draft/detail screens and controlled attachments.
2. Submission/review/domain transitions, required current-data gates and audit.
3. Internal integrations, ledger/handoff reconciliation, alerts and role-aware dashboards.
4. Verified optional external connectors, advanced reports and operational hardening.

Do not label an entire module complete after slice 1. Update the coverage tracker after each tested requirement.
